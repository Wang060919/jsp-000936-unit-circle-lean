## Historical mathematics

The unit-circle minimizer theorem is a historical mathematical result of
L. Brutman and A. Pinkus, with an earlier odd-node result of L. Brutman.
The trigonometric and global proof framework draws on work of C. de Boor,
A. Pinkus, and T. A. Kilgore. These mathematicians are credited for their
mathematical results, not listed as developers of this Lean project. The
bibliography appears in [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md).
